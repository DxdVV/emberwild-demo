# Audio implementation and evidence

The Audio autoload owns Music, SFX, UI, Ambience and Voice buses, settings application, music ducking and the active world's positional voice pool (at most 24). Cosmetic random selection/pitch does not consume gameplay RNG. Capture, dodge, loot and UI retain their existing samples. Generic cast/hit samples remain fallbacks for abilities without a profile and for periodic damage. Cue entries accept arrays; when several samples exist, the immediately preceding sample is excluded from the next random choice.

Trainer footsteps use three original, deterministic synthesized boot/earth contacts, generated by `tools/build_footsteps.py` and included in `tools/build_content.py`. They are mono 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz with 140–180 ms durations, short attacks and decaying low-frequency sole/noise components. No downloaded or third-party source audio is used. This is functional Foley, not final recorded sound design.

`CharacterAnimation.footfall` fires at the half/full boundaries of its six-pose walking cycle, after updating the visible pose. Resolved walking distance drives both animation and the contact signal. Blocked movement, idle, teleports, dodges, defeat and recovery do not create catch-up sounds. Moving casts keep walking and foot contacts. `ActorPresentation` routes the signal to the active world's SFX player at the trainer's current location. A -10 dB cue gain keeps footsteps below combat effects, in addition to the player's SFX volume setting. Pooled voices reset gain before other cues, so a reused footstep channel cannot make a hit quiet.

`tests/footstep_checks.gd` covers all four facing directions at 30/60 Hz, silent blocked/idle/teleported/dodging/defeated states, moving casts, recovery and variant selection. The full source test runner includes these checks. `tests/footstep_runtime.tscn` additionally drives real input, stops, pauses and resumes the session. Headless execution passes 24 checks. Native execution passes 26, including positional SFX stream/gain checks for every contact and reuse for a full-volume hit. `tools/test.ps1` includes the headless runtime.

Native evidence: `build/footsteps-native.log`, `build/footsteps-native.json`, and `build/footsteps.mp4` (229 frames at 60 FPS). The video includes game audio; an extracted gameplay frame was inspected. This is a behavior/recording check, not a frame-pacing benchmark or a claim of final audio quality.

All 14 authored abilities now reference an `AbilityAudioData` Resource. Six shared profiles distinguish fire, water, wood, electricity, the lantern and the guardian's heavy strike. Each has three cast and three impact samples (36 original synthesized WAVs), with separately editable cast/impact gains. `tools/build_combat_audio.py` generates the assets, Resources and ability assignments; the general content generator invokes it too. New audio assignments do not require species/element branches in gameplay. The content validator checks nested sample banks and finite gain bounds. Audio profiles are dependencies of the existing ability registry, not additional top-level registry entries.

The actual world launch hook plays cast audio after windup, not when input begins. Damage events select the ability's impact profile at the victim, including damage fully absorbed by a shield; invulnerability produces no false hit sound. Periodic damage keeps a quieter generic hit (-7 dB). A ground strike plays its heavy profile at the strike position even when it misses; the shared 28 ms per-cue rate limit coalesces immediate same-profile crowd hits. The existing 24-voice cap remains in force.

`tests/combat_audio_checks.gd` adds authored-profile, sample-variation and malformed-data coverage. `tests/combat_audio_runtime.tscn` actually casts all 14 abilities, waits for release and damage, and covers cancellation, shields, immunity and an empty ground strike. Native execution passes 95 checks, including stream, position and gain checks for each cast/impact and a 50-request same-profile burst producing one immediate voice. Evidence: `build/combat-audio-native.{log,json}`, empty `build/combat-audio-native.stderr.log`, and `build/combat-audio.mp4` (1,099 recorded frames at 60 FPS). The first fixture failure was an unsupported PackedStringArray.any call that skipped temporary-registry cleanup; it was corrected, and clean exit is required in addition to the final marker.

Still outstanding: surface-specific contacts, creature Foley, further sound-design polish, environmental profiles and final soundtrack/mixing acceptance. Footsteps currently represent the trainer's boots across the first area; flying companions do not play boot sounds. Synthesized elemental cues establish varied routing and timing, not final recorded audio quality.

Final headless combat-audio fixture count: 79. The full source suite passes 988 checks plus runtime subprocess scenarios. Portable build `20260926-123100-d354ec` includes these changes and passes extracted release startup; see `docs/distribution.md`. No stable-FPS or final-mix conclusion is drawn from Movie Maker timing.
